### Technical Purpose:
The diagram illustrates how a mechanical water lift system works. The spring compresses the ram, which then lifts water up the delivery pipe to the desired elevation within the house. The relief valve ensures that excess pressure does not build up in the system, preventing damage to the pipes and components. The air-water separator helps maintain the quality of the water by removing air bubbles before it reaches the household plumbing.
