Assume, for example, that the height to which the water is to be raised and the pressure required to operate the pump, including friction, make necessary a working pressure of 40 pounds on the pump. Then if the initial pressure in the air tank is 50 pounds, 187 gallons of water can be delivered at the faucets before the pressure in the air tank becomes too low to operate the pump.
