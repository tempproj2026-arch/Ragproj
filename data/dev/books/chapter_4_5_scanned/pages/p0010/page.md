This is a schematic diagram illustrating a water supply system, specifically a gravity-fed water distribution system used for both turbid water supply and pure water supply.

### Title:
The diagram does not have a specific title but is labeled as "TURBID WATER SUPPLY" on the left side and "PURE WATER SUPPLY" on the right side.

### Components:
1. **Power Water Level**: The highest point of the water source.
2. **Turbid Water Supply**: A pipe leading from the power water level to the gravity pipe.
3. **Gravity Pipe**: A horizontal pipe that carries turbid water from the power water level to the delivery valve.
4. **Delivery Valve**: A valve controlling the flow of turbid water into the body pipe.
5. **Body Pipe**: A vertical pipe that transports turbid water from the delivery valve to the check valve.
6. **Check Valve**: A valve that prevents backflow of turbid water.
7. **Gate Valve**: A valve used to control the flow of water through the delivery pipe.
8. **Spring Water Level**: The lowest point of the water source.
9. **Spring Pipe**: A pipe that carries water from the spring water level to the supply pipe.
10. **Supply Pipe**: A horizontal pipe that carries water from the spring pipe to the pure water supply outlet.
11. **Pure Water Supply**: The outlet where the pure water is distributed.

### Arrows:
- Arrows indicate the direction of water flow:
  - From the power water level to the gravity pipe.
  - From the gravity pipe to the delivery valve.
  - From the delivery valve to the body pipe.
  - From the body pipe to the check valve.
  - From the check valve to the gate valve.
  - From the gate valve to the supply pipe.
  - From the supply pipe to the pure water supply outlet.

### Inputs and Outputs:
- **Input**:
  - Power Water Level: Turbid water supply.
  - Spring Water Level: Pure water supply.
- **Output**:
  - Pure Water Supply Outlet: Pure water supply.

### Technical Purpose:
The diagram illustrates how turbid water is separated from pure water using a gravity-fed system. The turbid water flows from the higher power water level to the lower spring water level through a series of pipes and valves, ensuring that the pure water remains undisturbed at the spring water level.
