### Technical Purpose:
The sand filter is designed to purify water by allowing it to pass through layers of sand, which trap impurities. The settling tank and siphon chamber help in the separation of solids and gases from the water. The underdrains collect the filtered water, which then exits the system through the outlet. The diverting manhole allows for the diversion of water away from the main structure if necessary, possibly for maintenance or emergency purposes. The valve for cleaning out sludge is crucial for maintaining the efficiency of the filter over time.
